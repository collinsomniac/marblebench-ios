Inside the machine

24 mm solid marbles, gravity, collision surfaces, and a powered lift. The pool uses spherical-cap buoyancy and drag; its ripples are visual, not a full fluid solver. Funnel rolling resistance is approximated by angular damping. 

Waiting for the first frame.

High release rates are stress tests and can produce jams or escapes. Lost marbles are counted, never teleported.
